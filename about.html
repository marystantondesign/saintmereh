If you're reading this, we probably just met.

STORY TIME

I grew up in a small town, built a whole life in a different big city, but the goal was always to land in New York, permanently.

I have friends here that I knew before I moved, but whenever I meet a new person on my own, we end up following each other on Instagram and never actually hang out.

(I know there are a lot of reasons for that, but it still sucks, so I'm trying something different.)

This is my artist site. I built this for myself, but I'm sharing it with new friends because I want there to be a place beyond Instagram for new people to understand me and my work, to add on to your first impression.

I'm not trying to build a social media following or get funding for a creative project by connecting with you. I'm trying to actually get to know strangers who I meet and am interested in knowing. And if that leads to friendship of any kind, cool.

We probably met on the Lower East Side, so if you also live or work here, let's hang out, especially because I want to be friends with people in my neighborhood.

TLDR: if you're here, it's because I liked talking to you and I want to talk to you again, so let's do that.Let's subvert the usual expectations and actually hang out.

If you have my number, hit me up.

If you have my Instagram, shoot me a message.

Feeling brave? Give me a call. If I don't answer, leave me a voicemail.(Calling is a bold move, I'd be so impressed!)
